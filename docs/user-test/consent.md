# Consent (read this out before starting)

"Thanks for helping. I'm testing two versions of a tool, not you: if something is hard, that is the tool's fault.
It takes about 40 minutes. I'll time how long tasks take and take notes on what you do; I won't record audio or
video unless you say yes. Your answers are stored under an id like P3, never with your name. You can stop at any
time, and you can ask me to delete your data afterwards. Is that okay?"

Write down: participant id, date, "consent given: yes", and whether recording was allowed.
