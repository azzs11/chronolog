# ChronoBench: anomaly detection v2

Does a template-based detector beat the old rules and a plain severity rule? Generated by `uv run bench anomaly-v2`; do not edit by hand.

## Setup

- **Data:** Loghub HDFS_v1 and BGL (Zenodo record 8196385), first 2,000,000 lines of each. Units: HDFS blocks, 60 s BGL windows (same as `RESULTS-anomaly.md`).
- **Split by time:** the detector is fitted, without labels, on lines before the middle line's timestamp (first 50.0% of lines). Every method is scored only on TEST units, those whose first line is at or after that time, so the numbers differ from `RESULTS-anomaly.md`, which scores every unit.
- **v2 rare-template surprise:** a line is flagged if its Drain template made up less than 0.0001 of fit-period lines (or never appeared). Flag time: that line.
- **v2 Isolation Forest:** 100 trees on 256-unit subsamples of fit-period units, log(1 + template counts) per unit, anomalous if score > 0.5 (the defaults of Liu et al. 2008 / scikit-learn). Flag time: the unit's last line, because a unit is scored once complete.
- **Nothing was tuned:** every constant was fixed before the first run. The sensitivity table below was computed after seeing the results and did NOT change the headline setting.
- **Code:** v2 fingerprint `f2d8ac7c26c4d7f7`, ChronoLog-H fingerprint `cf431ae7b378ba2a`.

## HDFS

2,000,000 lines, 39 templates. Fit units 64,645; test units 43,402, of which 2,448 anomalous (5.6%).

| Method | Precision | Recall | F1 | Episodes detected | Median delay |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Always anomalous | 5.6% | 100.0% | 10.7% | 2448 of 2448 | +0 s |
| Random, ChronoLog-H's flag rate (5 seeds) | 2.9% | 0.4% | 0.6% (0.3%-0.8%) | 9 of 2448 | +2 s |
| Severity rule (error and critical lines) | 0.0% | 0.0% | 0.0% | 0 of 2448 | n/a |
| ChronoLog-H anomaly rules (v1) | 4.5% | 0.7% | 1.3% | 18 of 2448 | +1 s |
| **v2** rare-template surprise | 90.0% | 2.6% | 5.0% | 63 of 2448 | +0 s |
| **v2** Isolation Forest (5 seeds) | 6.1% | 93.6% | 11.4% (11.4%-11.5%) | 2291 of 2448 | +0 s |
| **v2** combined (either signal, 5 seeds) | 6.1% | 93.6% | 11.4% (11.4%-11.5%) | 2291 of 2448 | +0 s |

**Verdict (HDFS):** the best v2 variant (v2 Isolation Forest (5 seeds), F1 11.4%) beats the best trivial method (Always anomalous, 10.7%). ChronoLog-H v1: 1.3%. The seed-0 forest flags 11.5% of fit units and 85.4% of test units (a label-free check: a large gap means the two periods look different).
