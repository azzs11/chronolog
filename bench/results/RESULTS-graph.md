# ChronoBench: learned service graph

Can the service graph be learned from logs alone, and how close is it to the real call graph? Generated by `uv run bench graph`; do not edit by hand.

## Setup

- The graph is learned from each case's whole log window, with no incident start and no prior. A mention edge needs at least 3 naming lines; a correlation edge needs a correlation of at least 0.5 between per-2 s line counts.
- Edges are compared as undirected pairs against the call graphs of the two systems, written from their public architecture docs. They may omit real edges, which would count correct finds as false positives, so precision is a lower bound.
- **Train Ticket is not scored**: its 47-service call graph is not documented well enough to trust.
