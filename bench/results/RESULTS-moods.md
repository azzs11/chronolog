# ChronoBench: are the faces useful?

The app draws every service as a creature whose face is its mood (`mood.ts`: restarted, sick, stressed, gone quiet, new phrase, fine). Here those moods are scored on real incidents. Generated by `uv run bench moods`; do not edit by hand.

## How it was measured

- **Data:** all 359 RCAEval RE2/RE3 incidents, logs only. Templates are mined once per incident, as for RootRank. Moods compare each service's last 2 minutes with the 15 before; RCAEval windows start 12 minutes before the fault, so early steps have a shorter baseline than in the app.
- **Steps:** every 30 s from -60 s to +300 s around the fault. Nothing was tuned on these results: the mood rules and thresholds are the app's, written before this benchmark existed.
- **As a ranking:** services in the order they first look unwell after the fault (more serious mood first within a step). Ties are broken at random in expectation, never by name. Intervals are 95% cluster bootstrap over (system, root service) groups, like every other result.

## Results

| Method | AC@1 | AC@3 |
|---|---|---|
| Faces: first service to look unwell | 26.2% (14-39) | 52.9% (41-64) |
| RootRank (unseen services, 5 seeds) | 37.2% (27-47) | 52.6% (43-61) |
| First error | 7.2% (2-16) | 28.1% (16-41) |
| ChronoLog-H (old rules) | 7.8% (3-14) | 26.5% (14-40) |

- The broken service looks unwell within 5 minutes in **48.2%** of incidents, after a median of 30 s.
- Before anything broke (60 s before the fault) **4.1%** of services already looked unwell: the faces' false-alarm rate.
- By kind, AC@3: code faults 96.7% (90-100), resource faults 38.3% (27-51).

## Faces against RootRank, paired

## Caveats

- **Disclosure:** the moods have no training, so every incident is unseen by them. But their 'new phrase' mood uses RootRank's strongest signal (new message templates), which was found by the RootRank ablation over these same incidents. The idea, not any number, came from this data.
- The fault time is used only to choose when to look; the faces never see it.
  RootRank's headline also uses the true start; RESULTS-autostart.md has it with an estimated start.
